03 / Recording protocol

How to read
these demonstrations.

The six recordings compare the Native and Ours backends on three real-world tasks. Execution combines recorded demonstration trajectories with Cosmos-generated residual corrections.

These are qualitative demonstrations; task success was not formally evaluated in the run logs. The videos retain their supplied timelines, with audio and file metadata removed. Paired playback starts both recordings together, without aligning trajectories or normalizing their durations. The camera explorer includes the two available observation views.
