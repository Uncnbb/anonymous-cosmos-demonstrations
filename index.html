02 / Recording protocol

How to read
these demonstrations.

Successful trajectory examples of Cosmos3-Edge-Policy-DROID with a 20-step denoising schedule, comparing Native and Ours on flower arrangement, book placement, and plant watering.

Each clip begins at the estimated start of the first chunk inference. The main video and both camera observation streams share one playback timeline. The main-video alignment is approximate, based on robot motion events; camera snapshots retain their recorded relative timestamps and remain visible until the next capture. Playback speed changes all three views together.
